Four ways this could look

Same five things each time — stone, water, flour, fire, a cell. What separates these is not decoration: it is what the visual language claims about the game.

0 · What we have now

Emoji in a rounded pill on a warm gradient. This is the genre default and it is why it reads as a clone — Little Alchemy, Infinite Craft and two hundred imitators all look like this. A rock and a cake are the same shape and the same weight.

🪨Stone 💧Water 🥣Flour 🔥Fire 🦠Cell

A · Specimen tray

Every item is a catalogued specimen: a material swatch, a serif name, an accession number. The board is a dark collector's tray. This says these are real things, examined and recorded — which is exactly our claim, and the one thing no competitor can honestly make.

B · Field manual

Ink on graph paper. Objects are drawn, not photographed, with monospace captions and a plate number. Reads as a working notebook — the thing a person made while figuring the world out. Cheapest to produce consistently at 400 items.

C · Material only

No container at all. Each thing is rendered as the stuff it is, and the name sits quietly beneath. Nothing here is a "chip", so nothing reads as the competition.
