# Loam — brand

**Name:** Loam · **Pack one:** Dirt to Dinner

*Loam* is the soil that actually grows things — sand, silt and clay in the right
proportion, with enough life in it to hold water without drowning a root. It is
short, warm, easy to say and spell, unmistakably about origin rather than food
specifically, and it sounds like nothing else on the store. The game can grow
into biology, medicine and materials without the name fighting it.

## Icon — B2, "four into one"

`icon.svg` is the master, drawn on a 120-unit grid and exported at any size.

**What it says.** The four sources — stone, water, sun, seed — curve inward and
become one thing. The result is deliberately a *different shape* from its inputs:
a rounded rectangle, not a fifth dot. It is a thing that was made, not another
node in a network. That distinction is what stops the mark reading as a generic
tech logo.

**Why dark.** Almost every icon in the puzzle and merge categories is bright and
glossy. A warm near-black ground stands out by restraint, and it holds at 60px,
which is the size that actually decides it on a home screen.

## Still to check before anything is public

The name is chosen, not cleared. Three checks remain and none could be run in
the session that picked it:

1. **App Store search** for collisions on "Loam" in Games and Education.
2. **Trademark** search in the classes that matter (software, games).
3. **Domain** availability — `loam.game`, `playloam.com`, and the handle on the
   usual social platforms.

If Loam is taken in a way that matters, the runners-up were **Proof** (bread
proving, and proof as evidence — the thesis in one word) and **Stone Fruit** (a
real botanical category *and* a literal description of the game).
